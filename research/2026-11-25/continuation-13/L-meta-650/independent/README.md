# L-meta-650: independent restriction audit and bounded construction attempt

## Status

This checkpoint contains finite geometric analysis, a concrete obstacle to one cofactor-counting approach, and one targeted SAT search. It contains **no full proof, no 63-gate witness, and no official accepted submission** for L-meta-650. The SAT attempt finished UNKNOWN; the only fully evaluated construction has the already-known 64-gate size.

The exact task is the 26-input, 52-edge PG(2,3) incidence function in `../statement.txt`. The labels use `(p + l) % 13 ∈ {0,1,3,9}` for point `p` and line index `l`. This differs from the point-minus-line convention in earlier task L-meta-2718; mappings must be checked explicitly.

## Established finite observations

`geometry_audit.py` reproduces the geometric counts and the redundant-circuit control using ordinary Python. `geometry-summary.json` preserves compact counts and limitations; the full detailed JSON and control circuit are regenerated by the script. These computations have not been converted into new Lean certificates.

### No literal induced Heawood restriction

All 1,716 seven-point subsets were enumerated. At most six lines meet any such subset in exactly three points. An induced Heawood graph would require seven selected lines with exactly three selected point neighbors each. Thus the newly checked Heawood lower bound cannot be imported through a literal induced 14-variable graph restriction. This does not exclude identifications, other transformations, or a richer restriction argument.

### Four-star local repairs: existing-proof route

The following is a mathematical reduction using existing upstream lower bounds, not a newly compiled Lean theorem in this checkpoint. A standard four-point-star subfunction costs 19 gates. The three point configurations give:

- A quadrangle: subdivision-of-K4 core, with 10 vertices and 12 edges, plus four pendant line inputs. The existing L-meta-3516 core bound is 15; essential-leaf restriction adds one per leaf.
- A collinear triple plus an off-line point: a three-path theta core, with eight vertices and nine edges, plus seven pendant line inputs. The existing L-meta-516 core bound is 12, again giving 19 after essential leaves.
- Four collinear points: retain one private leaf per point and the common line. This is a four-arm subdivided star on nine vertices. Starting from the three-vertex path, three applications of `PEqualNP.Outside.Path575.terminal_pair` give a bound of 11; restoring eight further essential leaves gives 19.

The terminal-pair lemma requires a nonconstant previous function and false inputs with the attachment variable both false and true. Graph edge functions meet these conditions using an edge input, the zero input, and a singleton input. Exact relabeling and composition into a new formal theorem are not supplied here. This route blocks a saving confined to an isolated four-star replacement, while leaving larger replacements and cross-boundary sharing open.

## Six-cycle averaging route and its missing premise

There are 234 induced six-cycles, one for each noncollinear point triple. Each vertex belongs to 54 of them, and each pair of neighbors at a fixed vertex belongs to nine. The known eight-gate bound for a six-cycle corresponds to surplus at least three, using `surplus = gates - inputs + 1` for a strict live binary circuit. Summed over the cycles, this gives a required cofactor surplus sum of at least 702.

For a normalized minimum circuit, an upper bound of

```text
sum of suitable six-cycle cofactor surpluses <= 18 * original surplus
```

would imply original surplus at least 39 and hence at least 64 gates on 26 essential inputs. **That upper bound is unproved.** The new L-meta-971 restriction engine provides exact surplus accounting, but its decisive Heawood counting lemmas use triples on a universe of at most seven points. They do not directly yield the needed quartet argument on 13 points.

The script-generated `redundant-averaging-control.json` records a specific warning against applying the proposed upper bound to arbitrary circuits with an arbitrary fixed pruning method. It computes the full function once by point stars, once by line stars, then ORs the identical outputs. All 129 gates are structurally live and have earlier, distinct operands. Its surplus is 104. After the explicit local constant propagation, unary/duplicate-operand bypass, and dead-gate pruning in `geometry_audit.py`, each six-cycle cofactor has 17 gates and surplus 12. Their sum is 2,808, greater than `18 * 104 = 1,872`.

This control defeats that inequality for **this particular local cofactor procedure** on arbitrary structurally live circuits. The original circuit is deliberately nonminimum; the procedure also does not minimize its cofactors or identify semantically equal signals. The control therefore does not refute a bound restricted to minimum original circuits or a bound that allows independently minimum cofactors. Those distinctions are essential.

## One targeted constructive attempt

See `five-point-sat/README.md` for exact counts, hashes, and commands. It used the unmodified project encoding for 23 gates on the complete 18-input function formed by five points and all 13 lines. The boundary has 20 positive rows and 31 maximal negative rows. The CNF has 479,425 clauses and 5,033 used variables after a bijective renaming.

The single 300-second CaDiCaL run returned UNKNOWN. It neither found a witness nor proved nonexistence. The independently verified 24-gate local baseline extends by 40 gates to a full 64-gate circuit. Exhaustive evaluation checked all 262,144 local inputs and all 67,108,864 full inputs, with no errors. The results remain over budget.
